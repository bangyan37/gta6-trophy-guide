Roadmap to the Platinum — 5 Stages

Built from the official trophy descriptions: finish the story chapters and named missions first, then the strands, then sweep collectibles and exploration, then the activity grinds, and finish with money and 100% cleanup. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks when the other 54 are earned.

  1. Stage 1 — Story first

    Play the main story in order: Gone Fishing, Chapters 1–4, the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the final mission — trophies 2–13, including the final-mission gold. Save manually before the final mission and the VCIA visit (see cautions).

  2. Stage 2 — Strands & side missions

    Finish the named strands to their final missions — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone — plus the hunt for Bret. Whole strands build the 8 stranger strands for the silver Strangers & Freaks (trophies 22–27, 29).

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & exploration

    Sweep Leonida: 3 Collectible Card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas explored, all 18 meditation spots (trophies 28, 30, 31, 35, 42). Collect while travelling so exploration counts too.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities & grinds

    Clear the repeatable content: 250 stunts, gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, drone wins at all 14 locations, 20 different race wins, 50 challenges, each of the 9 activity types once (trophies 15–21, 32). Robberies and world moments — 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs), the partner wanted-level feats — belong here too.

  5. Stage 5 — Money & 100% cleanup

    Finish the economy: launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 across all shops, buy all 8 garage properties (trophies 52–54). Then check the tracker, reach 100% Completion for the gold A Long Road, and the platinum follows.

Missables & cautions — read this first

Only one trophy is flagged missable: Actions Speak Louder. Its official description, “Choices made (for better or worse)”, ties it to choices, so keep a manual save before major choices and reload to try another branch if it has not unlocked by the end of the story. No other description states a one-time requirement, so no other trophy is flagged — flagging more would be guessing.

Timing notes (not flagged missable): You Gotta Know When to Get Off — save before the final mission in case the ending changes free roam. Catch Me If You Can — try the unscheduled departure while you are at VCIA for the VCIA Takedown mission, and save before it. In The Beginning… — take the cocktail moment on Ocean Beach the first time it is offered. These cautions are general strategy, not claims that the trophies can be permanently missed.

Hidden trophies: in this guide the hidden flag follows the cryptic descriptions — trophies whose official description is a quote or riddle with no verb (Challenging Tank, In The Beginning…, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There). Their guides say plainly what the description does and does not confirm.

Online: there are no online trophies in the official list of 55 — every trophy is earned in single player per the official descriptions. The Online filter below says so honestly instead of inventing results.
